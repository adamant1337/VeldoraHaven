## What VeldoraHaven is

Premium outdoor-living brand. Categories: saunas, outdoor kitchens, cold plunges,
hot tubs, fire features. Current catalog centers on **Auroom** saunas (e.g. Halo, Luma
ranges). The store should feel like a **luxury architectural showroom**, not a generic
Shopify store.

## Positioning

Premium · architectural · sophisticated · minimal · natural · timeless · exclusive ·
high-quality · trustworthy · aspirational.

The experience must communicate, together:
**PRODUCT + QUALITY + LIFESTYLE + CRAFTSMANSHIP + SERVICE + CONFIDENCE + EXPERIENCE.**

## The buyer & the promise

High-ticket, high-consideration buyers. Every surface should quietly answer: Is this
high quality? Can I trust this company? Will it look good in my home? Will it last? How
do installation & delivery work? What if something goes wrong? Why is it worth the
price? Can I customize it? Can someone help me choose?

## Visual identity principles

Prefer: strong typography, generous deliberate whitespace, editorial/architectural
composition, high-quality photography, natural materials, restrained motion, high
perceived value.

**Signature direction (verified from the live theme, 2026-08-22):** a **cream page
canvas** (`#F7F7F5`) with **deep forest-green chrome** (header, nav, footer,
announcement bar — `#1B4332` / `#152E25`) and **muted gold accents** (`#C8A96E`) on
CTAs, logo, and links. Stone (`#3D3D35`) body text. Sharp corners (no rounded cards),
no shadows, serif display type over sans body. Exact tokens →
[`design-system/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`](../design-system/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

> Correction: an earlier note described a "whole-site dark green" look. The actual
> implemented theme is cream-bodied with green chrome + gold — not fully dark green.

Avoid: dropshipping/SaaS aesthetics, generic templates, excessive rounded cards /
gradients / shadows / animation, random colors, fake urgency, clutter, popup spam,
aggressive discount messaging, low-quality stock imagery.

## Verbal identity (voice)

Confident, calm, precise, understated. Sell craftsmanship and outcome, not hype. No
fake scarcity or discount-store language. Copy reads like a premium catalog / architect's
brief, not a performance-marketing landing page.

## The brand test (apply to every decision)

1. Does it feel like VeldoraHaven? 2. Is it premium? 3. Does it improve the customer
experience? 4. Does it support conversion? 5. Is it visually intentional? 6. Does it
work on mobile? 7. Does it stay performant?
