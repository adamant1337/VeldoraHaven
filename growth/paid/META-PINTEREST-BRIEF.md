## CREATIVE CONCEPTS

### Meta — 3 Hero Concepts

**Concept 1 — "The Evening Ritual" (lifestyle, cold audience)**  
Format: Video (15–30s Reel)  
Visual: Dusk exterior shot — lit sauna barrel in winter garden, steam rising. Cut to person exiting, breath visible in cold air, sitting on porch bench with coffee. Minimal sound design. Nordic ambient audio.  
Copy: "Some rituals are worth building a garden around."  
CTA: Shop Now → /collections/barrel-saunas  
Audience: Cold, 38–62, DK/DE/NL, homeowner signals

**Concept 2 — "Four Models, One Standard" (product range, consideration)**  
Format: Carousel (4 cards — Halo, Luma, Nora, Sola)  
Visual: Each card = one model hero image + model name + 1 key differentiator  
- Card 1: Halo — "Compact. Powerful. Perfect for smaller gardens."  
- Card 2: Luma — "The porch changes everything. Our most popular model."  
- Card 3: Nora — "Square barrel. Room for up to 6."  
- Card 4: Sola — "Our largest sauna. Built for the serious enthusiast."  
CTA: Shop Collection  
Audience: Website visitors + broad homeowner interest

**Concept 3 — "Every Question Answered" (trust, warm retargeting)**  
Format: Single image + overlay text  
Visual: Calm product shot — Luma exterior in a garden setting  
Copy: "Free EU delivery. DIY or pre-assembled. 2-year warranty."  
Body: "Most customers have one question before they buy: 'Is this actually worth it?' Speak with a specialist — no pressure, just answers."  
CTA: Book a Consultation → /pages/consultation  
Audience: PDP viewers, cart abandoners

### Pinterest — 3 Pin Concepts

**Pin 1 — "Garden Transformation" (aspirational, saves)**  
Format: 2:3 static image  
Visual: Wide-angle garden shot — Luma barrel sauna as focal centrepiece, surrounded by naturalistic planting, deck or stone path leading to it. Warm light, golden hour.  
Title: "How a barrel sauna transforms a garden | VeldoraHaven"  
Description: "An Auroom barrel sauna as the centrepiece of a designed outdoor space. Available in four finishes — natural, dark oil, light oil, and brushed black. Delivered across Europe. #BarrelSauna #OutdoorWellness #GardenDesign"

**Pin 2 — "Morning Ritual" (lifestyle, saves + engagement)**  
Format: Video Pin (20s loop)  
Visual: Time-lapse style — 7am light, steam from sauna, person entering, close-up of heater stones, exit + cold shower. Clean and aspirational.  
Title: "The morning routine worth waking up for"  
Description: "Barrel sauna → cold shower → first coffee. The Auroom Luma. Delivered across Europe. #SaunaLife #ScandinavianWellness #OutdoorSauna"

**Pin 3 — "Which Size?" (educational, clicks)**  
Format: 2:3 infographic-style image  
Visual: Clean comparison graphic — silhouette of 4 models with key dimensions and capacity  
Title: "Halo vs Luma vs Nora vs Sola: which barrel sauna fits your garden?"  
Description: "4 Auroom barrel sauna models compared by size, capacity, and best use case. Starting from €4,500 with EU delivery. Click to explore all configurations. #BarrelSauna #OutdoorSauna #GardenInspiration"
