## Auroom Halo vs Luma: Which Outdoor Barrel Sauna Is Right for You?

If you're narrowing down your choice to the Auroom Halo or the Auroom Luma, you're already in the right territory. Both are Estonian-manufactured barrel saunas built from Thermory thermo-spruce — a thermally modified wood that handles Northern European winters without warping, cracking, or greying. Both are CE certified. Both can be ordered as a DIY kit or delivered pre-assembled.

The difference comes down to one thing: the porch.

---

### The Halo — Pure Sauna, Refined Shape

The [Auroom Halo](/products/auroom-halo-barrel-sauna) is a classic outdoor barrel sauna in its most refined form. Seven sizes from Cosy 120 through to Extra Comfy, two finishes (Natural Wood and Brushed Black), and a clean cylindrical silhouette that works against almost any garden backdrop — a timber fence, a stone terrace, a garden edge backed by trees.

It's the model we recommend when the sauna is the destination. You go in, you heat up, you come out. The sessions happen inside. The Halo doesn't try to create an outdoor living moment around the sauna — it is the moment.

**What the Halo does well:**
- Smaller footprint. The Cosy 120 and Cosy 150 are compact enough for gardens where space is genuinely limited.
- Cleaner visual line. The unbroken cylinder reads as a sculptural garden object, particularly in Brushed Black.
- Lower entry price. The smallest Halo starts from €6,100 ex-VAT for the DIY kit — the most accessible way into the Auroom range.

**Who it's for:** Buyers who want a serious sauna that sits quietly in the garden. Couples, single bathers, smaller families. Gardens where you're not building an outdoor room around the sauna — you're placing an object in a space that already works.

---

### The Luma — With a Porch

The [Auroom Luma](/products/auroom-luma-barrel-sauna) is structurally the same sauna as the Halo, built to the same specification, from the same materials. The difference is the covered porch terrace that extends from one end of the barrel.

That porch changes how you use the sauna.

Instead of stepping out of the heat directly into your garden, you step onto a covered terrace. In summer, that terrace becomes the place where you cool down in the shade, wrap yourself in a towel, pour a cold drink. In the depths of a Danish or German November, it's the difference between an outdoor sauna session that feels brutal and one that feels deliberate.

The porch also changes how the Luma reads from the garden. It's longer, more architectural. It looks less like an object placed in a space and more like a destination that belongs there.

**What the Luma does well:**
- Creates a complete outdoor wellness zone. Sauna + covered cooling space in one unit.
- Better for contrast therapy. If you're cold plunging or taking cold showers between rounds, the porch gives you a transition zone.
- More social. Two people sitting on the porch between sessions is a different experience than two people standing in the garden.
- Works year-round without friction. The covered terrace makes use in autumn and winter far more comfortable.

**Who it's for:** Buyers who are building an outdoor ritual around the sauna, not just adding a sauna to a garden. Families, couples who entertain, anyone who wants to use the sauna through all four seasons. Gardens where there's space to let the Luma breathe.

### The Size Question

One thing worth flagging: if you need a compact sauna, the Halo is your only option between the two. The Luma's smallest configuration is the Cosy 225 — 225cm of barrel plus the porch extends the total length further. If your garden can take it, that's rarely a problem. But if you're working with a tight space, the Halo Cosy 120 or Cosy 150 are the models to look at.

If space isn't the constraint, we'd almost always recommend the Luma. The porch earns its cost in usability, especially if you're buying in a Northern European climate where sessions extend into October, November, and beyond.

---

### DIY Kit or Pre-assembled?

Both models are available as a flat-pack DIY kit or as a pre-assembled unit delivered and positioned by a two-person installation team.

The DIY kit is genuinely assembler-friendly — Auroom designs these to be built by two people over a weekend with basic tools. If you enjoy a build project and want to reduce the upfront cost, it's a credible option.

Pre-assembled costs more (the assembly fee is reflected in the price), but it removes all the logistics and labour. You confirm a delivery date, the team arrives, the sauna is positioned and ready. For buyers purchasing as a gift, for gardens with access constraints, or simply where your time is worth more than the saving, pre-assembled is the right call.

### Which One Should You Choose?

Choose the **Halo** if:
- You need a compact footprint
- You want the lowest entry price into the Auroom range
- The sauna is the event — you're not building an outdoor room around it
- You prefer the cleaner, unbroken barrel silhouette

Choose the **Luma** if:
- You have the garden space to give it room
- You want to use the sauna through autumn and winter without it feeling like an ordeal
- You're building a wellness ritual — sauna, cool-down, repeat
- You want the more complete outdoor experience in one unit

Both are exceptional saunas. The question is really what kind of experience you're building around them.
