**Current state:** 89% of 1,118 sessions = ~994 US sessions. Store is EUR/EU-only.  
**Impact:** Near-zero conversion potential on current traffic. The 0.45% add-to-cart rate comes almost entirely from non-purchasable traffic.  
**Action required (P1/P2):**
1. Confirm which EU markets are the primary targets (Denmark, Germany, France, Sweden, Netherlands, etc.)
2. Ensure Google Shopping campaigns geo-target EU only
3. Evaluate whether blog SEO content needs EU-market variants or hreflang
4. Do NOT interpret current 0% conversion as a store problem — it is a traffic problem
