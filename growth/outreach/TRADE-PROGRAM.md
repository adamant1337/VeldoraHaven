## PROGRAM OVERVIEW

VeldoraHaven's Trade Program offers preferential pricing and priority service to professionals who specify or supply premium outdoor wellness and living products to residential and commercial clients.

The program operates on a **dropship model** — no inventory required, no warehouse, no logistics. Trade partners specify, quote, and close the client; VeldoraHaven ships direct to the project site on their behalf.

---

## WHO QUALIFIES

**Primary targets:**
- Luxury landscaping and garden design firms
- Outdoor living contractors and pool/spa integrators
- Residential architects and interior designers specifying outdoor spaces
- Property developers (high-end residential, holiday let, boutique hospitality)

## PROGRAM BENEFITS

1. **Preferential trade pricing** — reduced from retail RRP on all Auroom barrel sauna models and accessories
2. **Direct-ship fulfilment** — products shipped from Auroom (Estonia) direct to your client's site; no stock holding required
3. **Priority order handling** — trade orders prioritised in Auroom's production queue where lead time flexibility exists
4. **Project support** — dedicated VeldoraHaven contact for specification questions, site suitability, and client-facing material
5. **Co-branded collateral** — product spec sheets and imagery available for your client presentations
6. **Lead times transparency** — Auroom standard: 3–6 weeks from order to delivery (varies by model and finish)

## APPLICATION PROCESS

**How to apply:**

2. Provide: company name, business type, VAT number, typical project scope
