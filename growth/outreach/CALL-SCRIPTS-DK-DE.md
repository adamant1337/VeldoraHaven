## THE LEGAL POSITION

### Denmark — calling is fine, email is not

**Markedsføringsloven § 10** bans unsolicited electronic mail, automated calling systems and fax for direct marketing without **prior consent**, and it covers businesses, not just consumers. The Consumer Ombudsman (Forbrugerombudsmanden) enforces it.

**Manual phone calls to businesses are not caught by § 10.** The restrictions on unsolicited calls target consumers. Cold-calling a Danish architecture practice is lawful.

**This is the key move:** end every call by asking *"må jeg sende jer sortimentet?"* — a yes is the prior consent that makes the follow-up email lawful. The call converts an illegal email into a legal one.

### Germany — calling is greyer, email is still out

**UWG § 7** requires prior **express** consent for email advertising, businesses included. That is a hard no for cold email.

For cold *calls* to businesses the standard is **mutmaßliche Einwilligung** — presumed consent. Lower bar than email, but still a bar: you need a concrete factual basis to assume the business would be interested. A practice that publishes wellness-garden and spa projects arguably meets it; a practice that only does public infrastructure does not.

**Practical read:** the three German firms below all publish relevant work, so a call is defensible. It is not as clean as Denmark. If you want to be conservative, use LinkedIn to open a conversation and ask permission to send material instead.

> Not legal advice. Worth 30 minutes with a Danish lawyer before scaling outbound, since this affects all 20 Danish prospects in the send-sheet, not just these four.

# DANISH CALL SCRIPT

**Structure:** reference their project → say why you're calling → the specification-partner offer → ask for consent to email.

### Opening

> "Hej, det er René fra Veldora Haven. Har du to minutter?
>
> Jeg ringer, fordi jeg har set jeres arbejde med **[PROJEKT]** — [én sætning om hvorfor det er relevant].
>
> Vi leverer Auroom udendørs saunaer fra Estland, og vi arbejder med tegnestuer som specifikationspartner frem for som butik: teknisk dokumentation og tegninger til projekteringsfasen, levering direkte til bygherrens adresse, og ingen lagerføring eller håndtering hos jer.
>
> Hvor et projekt har et wellnesselement — gårdrum, tagterrasse, hotel eller bolig — er en sauna en af de få udendørs ting, der bliver brugt hele vinteren."

### The consent close — do not skip this

> "**Må jeg sende dig sortimentet og de tekniske specifikationer, så du har det liggende?**"

A yes is your consent under § 10. Log it — date, who said it, what they agreed to receive. Then send the held draft.

If no: *"Det er helt fint — tak for tiden."* Mark as declined and do not email.
