## 1. EU Right of Withdrawal (Consumers)

If you are a consumer located in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within **14 days** of receiving the last item in your order, without giving any reason.

To exercise this right, contact us at **info@veldorahaven.com** before the withdrawal period expires, including your order number and a clear statement that you wish to cancel.

This right applies to consumer purchases within the EU only. See Section 7 for business customers and Section 8 for customers outside the EU.

## 2. Returning Large or Freighted Items

Many of our products (saunas, outdoor kitchens, cold plunges, hot tubs) ship as heavy, palletized freight. Standard parcel-return rules don't apply, so:

- **We arrange the return freight for you.** Once you've notified us of your withdrawal, we'll coordinate pallet pickup through our carrier network rather than asking you to book your own freight.
- **Estimated return shipping cost is disclosed before you commit.** Return freight is charged to the customer per EU law, but we will provide a specific cost quote (typically **€150–€600 depending on product size and destination**) before you finalize the return, so there are no surprise charges. *[Replace range with real carrier quotes before publishing.]*
- Items must be returned in their original packaging where possible, unassembled, and in resaleable condition. See Section 4.

## 3. Transit Damage & Delivery Inspection (Not a Withdrawal — Report Immediately)

Damage discovered on arrival is handled separately from a change-of-mind return, and the timing matters:

- **Inspect the delivery before signing.** Check all visible surfaces and packaging at the time of delivery.
- **Report any visible damage on the delivery note and to us within 48 hours**, with photographs of the damage and packaging.
- **Do not install or assemble a visibly damaged product.** Per our supplier's warranty terms, damage that was visible before installation but not reported before installation is not covered.
- Once reported, we will arrange repair, replacement of parts, or a full remedy at no cost to you — this does not count against your 14-day withdrawal window.

---

## 4. Refunds

- We refund all payments received, including standard delivery costs, within **14 days** of receiving the returned item(s) back (or of receiving proof that you've shipped it, if earlier).
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- **Deduction for diminished value:** if the product shows signs of use, assembly, weather exposure, or handling beyond what's reasonably needed to inspect it, we may deduct the corresponding loss in value from your refund, as permitted under EU law.
- Return freight cost (Section 2) is deducted from the refund unless the return is due to a defect or transit damage (Section 3), in which case it is covered by us.

---

## 5. Manufacturer Warranty (Separate from Returns)

In addition to your statutory rights, sauna and sauna-related products carry the manufacturer's warranty:

- **Duration:** 2 years from the date of delivery to a consumer customer, capped at 3 years from the date of manufacture.
- **Covers:** manufacturing and material defects that prevent intended use.
- **Does not cover:** improper installation, use contrary to instructions, unsuitable storage (weather/temperature/humidity exposure), damage occurring during installation or use, natural wood characteristics (grain, knots, tone variation), or visible transport damage not reported before installation.
- **To claim:** stop using the product immediately, and contact us with the order/delivery date, a description of the defect, and photographs. You have up to 2 months from becoming aware of a defect to notify us. We aim to respond within two weeks of escalating to the manufacturer.
- This warranty applies independently of, and in addition to, the 14-day withdrawal right above — a defect discovered after 14 days is a warranty matter, not a return.

---

## 6. Exceptions to the Right of Withdrawal

The right of withdrawal does not apply to:

- **Bespoke/custom-built products** — items manufactured to your unique specification beyond our standard published configurations (dimensions, finishes, and add-ons chosen from our standard product options are *not* considered custom-made and remain returnable).
- Sealed goods not suitable for return for health or hygiene reasons, once unsealed after delivery.
- Digital content that has been accessed or downloaded.

---

## 7. Business (B2B) Customers

The 14-day EU consumer withdrawal right applies to consumers only and does not extend to business/trade purchases. Business orders are governed by our separate trade terms, which cover:

- Delivery terms and risk transfer (freight/incoterms-based)
- Inspection and non-conformity reporting windows (report within 7 calendar days of discovering, or reasonably discovering, a defect)
- Manufacturer warranty pass-through (Section 5 terms apply equivalently)
- Payment terms and deposit requirements for custom/large orders

Contact us at info@veldorahaven.com for our current trade terms document before placing a business order.
