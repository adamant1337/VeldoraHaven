Return & Refund Policy

EU Right of Withdrawal

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving the last item in your order, without giving any reason.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, please contact us at info@veldorahaven.com before the withdrawal period expires. Please include your order number and a clear statement that you wish to cancel your order. You can also start a return request directly from your account: https://shopify.com/102956204374/account

Returning your order

Once you have notified us of your withdrawal, you must return the item(s) to us without undue delay and no later than 14 days after notifying us.

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer. For large or palletized items (such as saunas and outdoor kitchens), we arrange the return freight through our carrier network on your behalf and will provide you with the exact return shipping cost before you commit to sending the item back, so there are no surprise charges.

Refunds

We will refund all payments received from you, including standard delivery costs, within 14 days of receiving the returned item(s), or from the day you provide proof that you have sent the item back, whichever is earlier. Refunds will be issued using the same payment method you used for the original purchase.

If the returned item shows signs of use, assembly, weather exposure, or handling beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it, we may deduct the corresponding loss in value from your refund, as permitted under EU law.

Damaged or defective items

If your item arrives damaged, please check it at the point of delivery and note any visible damage on the delivery note. Report damage to us within 48 hours at info@veldorahaven.com with photographs of the item and packaging. Please do not install or assemble a visibly damaged product. Once reported, we will arrange a repair, replacement, or full remedy at no cost to you, and this does not count against your 14-day withdrawal window.

Manufacturer warranty

Sauna and sauna-related products also carry the manufacturer's warranty, valid for 2 years from the date of delivery (capped at 3 years from the date of manufacture), covering manufacturing and material defects. This warranty does not cover improper installation, use contrary to instructions, unsuitable storage, damage during installation or use, or natural wood characteristics such as grain, knots, or tone variation. To make a claim, stop using the product and contact us with your order number, delivery date, a description of the defect, and photographs, within 2 months of noticing the issue. This warranty applies in addition to, and independently of, your right of withdrawal above.

Supplier-specific return conditions

As we work with multiple suppliers, return conditions may vary depending on which supplier fulfilled your order. Any supplier-specific conditions will be clearly communicated at the time of your return request and will always meet or exceed the minimum standards required under EU law.

Business (trade) customers

The 14-day EU consumer withdrawal right described above applies to consumer purchases only and does not extend to business or trade orders. Business orders are governed by our separate trade terms covering delivery, inspection windows, and warranty pass-through. Contact us at info@veldorahaven.com for our current trade terms.

Exceptions

The right of withdrawal does not apply to:

* Bespoke or custom-built products manufactured to your unique specification beyond our standard published configurations. Choosing a size, finish, or add-on from our standard product options does not count as custom-made and remains returnable.
* Sealed goods that are not suitable for return due to health or hygiene reasons, if unsealed after delivery.
* Digital content that has been accessed or downloaded.
