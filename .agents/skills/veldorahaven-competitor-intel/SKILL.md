# VELDORAHAVEN COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE

Deliver a sharp, actionable competitor snapshot that directly informs a pricing,
positioning, or category decision — not an academic market survey.

---

## BRAND CONTEXT

VeldoraHaven — premium outdoor-living. Categories: outdoor kitchens, saunas (barrel,
cabin, infrared), cold plunges, hot tubs/swim spas, fire features. Positioning:
architectural, minimal, timeless, aspirational. Price tier: upper-mid to ultra-premium.
Geography: primarily Scandinavia + Western Europe, growing UK/US interest.

The useful question is always: **where does VeldoraHaven have a right to win?**

---

## RESEARCH PROCESS

### 1. Scope the request (30 seconds)
Identify:
- **Category**: which product type?
- **Geography**: which market?
- **Decision**: what will this research directly inform? (pricing / copy / supplier choice / gap analysis)

### 2. Run focused web searches
Use 4–6 targeted searches. Prefer:
- Brand + product category + price (e.g. "Klafs sauna outdoor price EUR 2025")
- Category + "luxury" or "premium" + country
- Specific competitor domains for pricing pages

Good starting competitors by category (verify — the market moves):
- **Saunas**: Klafs, Tylö, Harvia, Almost Heaven, dundalk leisurecraft, Finnleo, Auroom (own brand)
- **Cold plunge**: Renu Therapy, Ice Barrel, Plunge, Invigor8, Lumi Recovery Pod
- **Hot tubs/swim spas**: Jacuzzi, Villeroy & Boch, Sundance, Master Spas, Wellis
- **Outdoor kitchens**: Röshults (own brand), Napoleon, Kalamazoo, Twin Eagles, Hestan, Bulthaup outdoor
- **Fire features**: Hase, MCZ, Planika, GlammFire, Harrie Leenders

## QUALITY STANDARDS

- Report only what you found, not what you assume. If pricing is not publicly available, say so.
- Distinguish list price from street price where visible.
- Flag if a competitor's info is >12 months old.
- Keep the whole report under 600 words — this is a decision tool, not a thesis.
- If the category has <3 real competitors findable online, say so and explain why (niche market, opaque pricing, etc.)
