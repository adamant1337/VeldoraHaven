# VELDORAHAVEN BRAND DUE DILIGENCE

Produce a decision-ready brand brief that tells VeldoraHaven whether to pursue a
partnership, at what terms, and what to watch out for. Think: investment memo, not
press release.

---

## CONTEXT

VeldoraHaven carries carefully curated brands in outdoor living. Every brand on the
site is a signal about who VeldoraHaven is. The question is never just "is this brand
good?" — it's "does this brand make VeldoraHaven stronger?"

Distributor relationships are complex: exclusivity, e-commerce rights, freight zones,
margin structure, and brand support all matter. Research should surface the factors
that will appear in negotiation.

---

## RESEARCH PROCESS

### 1. Establish the basics (brand.com + quick searches)
- Brand website: products, pricing, materials, design language
- Founded, HQ, ownership (private/PE/corporate)
- Target market and price positioning
- Current distribution model (direct only? dealer network? geographic exclusivity?)

### 2. Product & quality research
- Core product lines and price ranges
- Materials, certifications, country of manufacture
- Flagship products and what they're known for
- Any awards, press coverage, notable clients

### 3. Market reputation
- Customer reviews (Google, Trustpilot, Reddit, forums)
- Trade press mentions
- Social media presence and quality (Instagram, Pinterest — visual brands matter here)
- Any known quality issues or service complaints

### 4. Distribution landscape
- Who currently distributes them in target markets?
- Are they direct-to-consumer? Dealer-only?
- Any geographic gaps (markets where they have no presence)?
- Do they allow e-commerce, or is it brick-and-mortar only?
- Any known exclusivity arrangements?

### 5. Fit assessment
- Price tier alignment with VeldoraHaven (upper-mid to ultra-premium)
- Design language fit (architectural, minimal, timeless?)
- Category fit (sauna, outdoor kitchen, cold plunge, etc.)
- Likely margin structure (premium brands often 40–55% dealer margin — verify)

## QUALITY STANDARDS

- Be direct about gaps: if pricing is hidden, say so and explain what that likely means.
- Distinguish facts (found on their site) from signals (inferred from reviews/positioning).
- Flag anything that conflicts with VeldoraHaven's premium positioning without softening it.
- Keep under 700 words — this is a go/no-go tool.
